# Privacy Policy — Banknote Assistant

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Banknote Assistant ("the app") helps blind and visually impaired people count Bangladeshi
banknotes using the phone's camera and voice. This policy explains what the app does with
information on your device.

## Summary
- No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs.
- By default the app works **on your phone**: photos of banknotes are never uploaded.
- Banknote photos are temporary and are deleted automatically.
- Your counting history (amounts only) stays on your phone and can be deleted at any time.

## Camera
The camera is used only to see the banknote you are scanning. Each photo is kept in the app's
private temporary storage for the current scan only. It is deleted when you scan the next note,
when the session ends, and every time the app starts. Photos are never saved to your gallery.
The app does not read or store banknote serial numbers.

## Microphone and speech
The microphone is used only while the app is visible and listening for commands such as
"Start" or an amount such as "70 Taka". Speech is converted to text by your phone's built-in
speech recognition service, for example Google speech services. That service may process audio
on its provider's servers under its own privacy policy, unless you turn on
**Settings → Prefer offline speech recognition** and your phone supports offline recognition.
The app does not record or store audio. Listening stops when the app goes to the background.

## Data stored on your device
- Counting sessions: note values, the number of notes, the required payment amount, and
  calculated balances, with dates. These are stored in a private database on your phone.
- Settings: language, speech rate and recognition mode.

This data is not backed up to the cloud by the app and is not shared with the developer. You
can delete saved sessions in **Settings → Saved sessions**, or delete everything by clearing
the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Optional server mode
If you choose **Settings → Recognition → Server**, you must first agree to a disclosure. Each
banknote photo is then sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the server address **you**
enter. The reference server software processes the photo in memory and keeps only the numeric
result (denomination, confidence and torn percentage), never the photo. If you use a server
operated by someone else, that operator's privacy practices apply. You can turn server mode
off at any time.

## Permissions
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | To scan banknotes |
| Microphone | For voice commands and spoken amounts |
| Internet | Only for the optional server mode |
| Vibration | For haptic feedback |

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from
anyone.

## Limitations
The app only helps you calculate cash amounts. It does not detect counterfeit notes, verify
authenticity, or make or confirm payments.
